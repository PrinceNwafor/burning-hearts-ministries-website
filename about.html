Our Story

We Are Burning Hearts Ministries

A ministry born from a mandate to set hearts on fire for God — from Lagos, Nigeria to the ends of the earth.

Founded with a Mandate

How It All Began

Burning Hearts Ministries (BHM) was founded in April 2016 by Pastor Elvis Okhifo — a firebrand teacher and preacher of the gospel of Christ who received a clear mandate from God: "To set hearts on fire for God all over the world."

What began as a small gathering of believers hungry for the authentic move of God has grown into a thriving, fire-filled community in Ikeja, Lagos — and a global ministry reaching nations through online platforms, campus outreach, and prophetic ministry.

Today, BHM holds Sunday services at EridanSpace in Ikeja, Lagos, and reaches thousands globally through YouTube, podcasts on Apple Podcasts and Spotify, and the ROOTED campus outreach program.

Leadership

Meet Our Leadership

Anointed, fire-filled, and deeply committed to seeing every person walk in their God-given destiny.

Pastor Elvis Okhifo
Founder & Lead Pastor

Pastor Elvis Okhifo is a firebrand teacher and preacher of the gospel of Christ — a Bible teacher, prophetic voice, musician, pianist, life and business coach, educator, and CEO of Stofel Africa.

Called by God with a clear mandate, he founded Burning Hearts Ministries in April 2016 with one burning conviction: that the same fire that sent the disciples running from Emmaus — hearts ablaze after encountering the risen Christ — must be the experience of every believer today.

He is the author of "Whispers of Fire" and hosts a widely followed podcast on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and Audiomack. Pastor Elvis ministers with a rare blend of prophetic insight, pastoral warmth, and fiery scriptural accuracy.

Pastor Charis Elvis-Okhifo

Pastor Charis serves alongside her husband as Co-Pastor of Burning Hearts Ministries. With a heart for pastoral care, discipleship, and building authentic community, she is a pillar of warmth and wisdom in the BHM family.

Together, Pastor Elvis and Pastor Charis lead BHM with a unified vision — building a church that looks like heaven, sounds like revival, and feels like home.
