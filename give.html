The Heart Behind Giving

Why We Give at BHM

At BHM, giving is not about funding a building or a program — it is an act of worship. When you give, you are partnering with the mission of God to set hearts on fire in every nation.

Your generosity fuels Sunday services, the ROOTED Campus Outreach, global missions, and the online ministry that reaches thousands weekly through YouTube, Spotify, and Audiomack.

Questions About Giving

Giving FAQs

Is my giving tax-deductible?

Please contact us directly to discuss tax receipt arrangements for your gifts to BHM.

Can I set up a recurring monthly gift?

Yes. Reach out to us via WhatsApp or email and we'll set up consistent giving arrangements for you.

Can I give from outside Nigeria?

Absolutely. BHM has supporters across 20+ nations. Contact us to get international giving details.

What percentage of my gift goes to the mission?

BHM is committed to stewarding every gift faithfully. We operate with transparency and integrity — the mission always comes first.
