Our Foundation

What We Believe

Faith that is accurate, rooted in Scripture, alive in the Spirit, and transforming in everyday life.

At Burning Hearts Ministries, our beliefs are not a list of religious rules — they are the living convictions that set hearts ablaze. We are a charismatic, Spirit-filled community that upholds the full counsel of Scripture, believes in the present-day work of the Holy Spirit, and walks in the prophetic fire of God's Word.

Statement of Faith

Our Core Beliefs

Grounded in the Word. Alive in the Spirit. Burning for God.

01
The Bible

We believe the Bible is the fully inspired, inerrant Word of God — the supreme authority for all matters of faith and life. Every sermon at BHM is built on the accurate teaching of Scripture.

2 Timothy 3:16–17
02
The Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existing as three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. God is holy, loving, just, and profoundly personal. He is not distant — He is near.

Deuteronomy 6:4 · Matthew 28:19
03
Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus is fully God and fully human. He was born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, rose bodily from the dead, and will return in glory and power.

John 1:1,14 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–4
04
The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit is active today — dwelling in every believer, releasing gifts, producing fruit, empowering for service, and setting hearts on fire. The gifts of the Spirit are for the church now.

Acts 1:8 · 1 Corinthians 12:4–11
05
Salvation by Grace

We believe every person is made in God's image but fallen through sin. Salvation is a free gift — received through faith in Jesus Christ alone, not through human effort, tradition, or ritual.

Ephesians 2:8–9 · Romans 3:23
06
Divine Ordination

We believe every believer has a God-given divine ordination — a purpose and calling that God wants them to walk in victoriously. Discovering and fulfilling this calling is central to the BHM vision.

Jeremiah 29:11 · Ephesians 2:10
07
Prophetic Ministry

We believe the prophetic gift is active in the church today. God still speaks through anointed voices to bring direction, encouragement, correction, and revelation to his people.

1 Corinthians 14:3 · Amos 3:7
08
Healing & Restoration

We believe God heals — spirit, soul, and body. The ministry of healing is part of the gospel of the Kingdom. We pray for the sick and believe God's power is available for total restoration today.

Isaiah 53:5 · James 5:14–15
09
The Global Church

We believe in the global body of Christ — one Church across nations, cultures, and languages. BHM is called to take the fire of God's presence to all peoples, all nations, and all generations.

Matthew 28:19 · Revelation 7:9

"For the word of God is alive and active. Sharper than any double-edged sword,
it penetrates even to dividing soul and spirit."

Hebrews 4:12

Who We Are

Fire Without Compromise

We are a non-denominational, charismatic church. We hold to the full authority of Scripture while embracing the present-day work of the Holy Spirit — including prophecy, tongues, healing, and miracles.

We welcome seekers, the broken, the questioning, and the hungry. We believe God is not afraid of your doubts — but He does have answers that will set your heart on fire.

If religion has disappointed you but you're still looking for the real God — you are exactly who we want to sit with.

Common Questions

Things People Often Ask

Do I have to be a Christian to attend BHM?

Not at all. We welcome people at every stage of faith. Come, explore, ask questions. We believe in a God who meets you wherever you are — and we want to be a church that does the same.

What denomination is BHM?

We are a non-denominational, charismatic church. We believe in the full gospel of Jesus Christ, the authority of Scripture, and the present-day power of the Holy Spirit. We are not defined by a label — we are defined by the fire.

Do you believe in spiritual gifts and prophecy today?

Yes. We believe the gifts of the Holy Spirit — including prophecy, tongues, healing, and miracles — are for the church today. We operate in these gifts with wisdom, scriptural accuracy, and accountability.

What do you mean by "divine ordination"?

We believe God has a specific purpose and calling for every person's life — a divine ordination they were created to walk in victoriously. At BHM, we teach and equip believers to discover and step into that purpose through the power of the Word and the Spirit.

How do I learn more about what BHM believes?

The best way is to come on a Sunday and experience it. You can also listen to Pastor Elvis's sermons on YouTube (@bhm_global), Apple Podcasts, or Spotify. Or reach out directly — we'd love to connect with you.
