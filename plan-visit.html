What Happens on Sunday

What to Expect

From the moment you walk in, you'll feel the warmth of a community that genuinely loves God and loves people.

1
A Warm Welcome

From the moment you arrive, our hospitality team will greet you warmly. First-timers are always acknowledged — but never put on the spot. You can settle in without pressure.

2
Powerful Worship

BHM worship isn't just music — it's an encounter with God. Led by a passionate team, our worship sets the atmosphere for the presence of God to move freely in the room.

3
Accurate Bible Teaching

Pastor Elvis Okhifo brings the Word of God with precision, fire, and transforming anointing every week. Messages are rooted in scripture, relevant to life, and delivered with clarity.

4
An Altar Call & Prayer

Every service includes an opportunity to respond to God — whether for salvation, rededication, healing, or personal prayer. No one is forced, but the door is always open.

5
Community & Connection

After service, take time to meet people. The BHM community is warm, genuine, and growing. Many people who came for one Sunday have never stopped coming back.

Your Host

You'll Be Taught by
Pastor Elvis Okhifo

Pastor Elvis is a firebrand preacher, Bible teacher, and prophetic voice who has been setting hearts on fire since founding BHM in 2016. Known for accurate, anointed teaching that transforms lives, he leads every Sunday service with passion and precision.

Whether you're a long-time believer or stepping into a church for the first time, his messages meet you right where you are.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to dress a certain way?

No dress code. Come as you are. We care far more about your heart than your outfit.

Is there a children's program?

Families are welcome to the main service together. Reach out via WhatsApp before your visit and we'll let you know about current arrangements for children.

What if I arrive late?

No problem. You're welcome to join us at any point during the service. Our doors are always open.

Will I be asked to give money?

An offering is taken during service, but participation is completely voluntary for first-time visitors. No pressure, ever.

Have a Question Before You Come?

Our team is happy to answer anything — via WhatsApp, email, or phone. We want you to feel fully prepared and comfortable.
