Campus Ministry

ROOTED: Setting Campuses on Fire

ROOTED is BHM's campus outreach program — taking the fire of God's Word directly onto university campuses across Nigeria. We believe the next generation is not a future project; they are the present answer.

Currently active at Lagos State University (LASU), ROOTED is expanding. If you're a student, a campus ministry leader, or an alumni who wants to bring BHM to your campus — we want to hear from you.
